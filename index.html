Customer Churn Prediction Web App

A machine learning web app that predicts whether a telecom customer will leave. You enter customer details, and it shows a high or low risk result with a percentage. You can also download the result as a PDF report.

I cleaned the data with Pandas, stored it in SQLite, and removed columns such as Churn Reason that would leak the answer to the model.
